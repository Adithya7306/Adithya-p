Sales Data Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Analyzed a sales dataset to understand business performance, identify trends, compare product categories and regions, and explore relationships between sales, profit, and discounts. The project focuses on data cleaning, exploratory analysis, summary statistics, and data visualization to produce useful business insights.

Technologies Used:
Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib Seaborn Jupyter / Google Colab

Project Objectives & Scope:

  • Inspect the dataset and understand its structure.
  • Identify and handle missing values and inconsistent data where necessary.
  • Explore numerical and categorical variables.
  • Analyze sales performance by category, product, and customer segment.
  • Compare regional performance.
  • Identify high-performing categories and regions using the available data.
  • Examine sales and profit trends over time if date information is available.
  • Investigate the relationship between discounts, sales, and profit.
  • Visualize findings using appropriate charts.
  • Summarize findings and provide practical business recommendations supported by the analysis.
